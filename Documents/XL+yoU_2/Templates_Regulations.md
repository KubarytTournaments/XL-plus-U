<h1 align="center">Template Regulations</h1>

## 1. Templates applicable in the Tournament

1. For the Tournament the following templates are applicable:

    - 6lm10f fixed,
    - 8xm12a 1.1,
    - Nostalgia 1.1,
    - Sapphire XL,
    - Spider 2.2a.

2. Link to download all the above-mentioned templates is available on [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wWTXVdHCuU9YI7Fo_ZugGh1abWo_wgMs?usp=sharing).

3. By mutual agreement of both Participants, the Game may be played on the following template versions:

    - for 6lm10f fixed:
      - 6lm10 1.1,
      - 6lm10a,
    - for 8xm12a 1.1:
      - 8xm12a,
    - for Nostalgia 1.1:
      - Nostalgia,
    - for Sapphire XL:
      - Sapphire XH,
    - for Spider 2.2a:
      - Spider.

## 2. Default settings of templates in Games

1. By mutual agreement of both Participants, the timer settings, simultaneous turns, or the number of AI can be changed.
