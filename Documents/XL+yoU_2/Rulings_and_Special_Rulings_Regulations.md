<h1 align="center">Rulings and Special Rulings Regulations</h1>

0. Each time the Tournament Rules mention a Ruling, it is understood to mean a Ruling or a Special Ruling, unless otherwise stated.

## 1. Method of Issuing Rulings

1. <a id="point-1-1"></a>In the case of an individual decision, the member of the Committee making it issues a Ruling by publishing it on Github or Discord or by providing information about it to another member of the Committee, who issues a Ruling indicating who made the decision.

2. In the case of a majority vote, the Committee Chairman issues a Ruling as described in [point 1. of this chapter](#point-1-1).

3. In the case of a Participant's decision, e.g. withdrawal from the Tournament, the Committee member who received information about it issues a Ruling as described in [point 1. of this chapter](#point-1-1).

4. <a id="point-1-4"></a>Decisions that can be made by Ruling:

    `1)` Punishing a Participant in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `2)` Considering an appeal against a penalty in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `3)` Setting a deadline for a given phase in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `4)` Changing the deadline for a given phase or Match in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `5)` Changing the system of playing a Match, which according to the relevant point of the Regulations should be played in a Bo3 or Bo5 system, to a Bo1 or Bo3 system,\
    `6)` Interpreting an unclear part of the Tournament Rules,\
    `7)` Information about the actions of Participants. E.g.:\
    <ul style="list-style: none;">
    <li>a) Withdrawal from the Tournament,</li>
    <li>b) Giving a walkover in a Game,</li>
    <li>c) Giving a walkover in a Match,</li>
    </ul>
    `8)` Making a decision on an issue not covered by the Tournament Rules, in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `9)` Ending a Match by a coinflip, in accordance with the procedure set out in the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `10)` Allowing the non-broadcasting of Games in a phase where it is mandatory,\
    `11)` Establishing the order in a group based on a coinflip,\
    `12)` Announcing the results of overtimes.

5. <a id="point-1-5"></a>Decisions that can only be made by Special Ruling:

    `1)` Approving new versions of the Tournament Rules,\
    `2)` Establishing, changing the composition of, and dissolving the Referee Committee,\
    `3)` Giving thanks and congratulations to Participants, commentators, or hosts of the broadcast of a Game played as part of the Tournament and members of the Committee,\
    `4)` Awarding first, second, and third place in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `5)` Revoking a Ruling, Special Ruling, or other Document listed in the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `6)` Revoking a decision of the Committee or a Committee member, excluding appeals against decisions in accordance with the relevant point of the Regulations,\
    `7)` Allocating Committee members to specific areas of the Tournament,\
    `8)` Establishing a rule applicable in the Tournament, without updating other Tournament Rules.

## 2. Ruling Shape

1. Ruling consists of Ruling Number, Ruling Content, and information about the person issuing Ruling.

2. Ruling Number consists of a $ sign, 2 digits, a hyphen, 2 digits, and the letter X (e.g. $02-01X). The first two digits indicate in which edition of the Tournament the Ruling is valid, and the next two digits indicate the ordinal number of the Ruling. The higher the ordinal number of the Ruling, the greater the power it has, so if Ruling $02-01X is inconsistent with Ruling $02-06X, then Ruling $02-06X is valid. In the case of a Special Ruling, instead of the $ sign, there is a # sign in the Ruling Number.

3. In the case of a decision mentioned in paragraphs `2)`, `4)`-`6)`, and `10)` [point 4. of chapter 1.](#point-1-4), the Ruling should also contain information about the applicant.

4. In the case of a decision mentioned in paragraph `7)` [point 4. of chapter 1.](#point-1-4), the Ruling should contain information about the Participant who made the decision.

5. Ruling Content is divided into points (not all points listed must be included in each Ruling):

    `1)` Withdrawals - informs about the withdrawals of Participants,\
    `2)` Walkovers in a Game - informs about Participants who have voluntarily given up Games,\
    `3)` Walkovers in a Match - informs about Participants who have voluntarily given up Matches,\
    `4)` Appeals - informs about Participants' appeals against Committee decisions and their consideration,\
    `5)` Penalties and reprimands - informs about penalties imposed on Participants,\
    `6)` Schedule - informs about the dates of phases,\
    `7)` Extending deadlines - informs about decisions from paragraph `4)` [point 1. of this chapter](#point-1-4),\
    `8)` Changing the system of playing Matches - informs about decisions from paragraphs `5)` and `9)` [point 4. of this chapter](#point-1-4),\
    `9)` Overtimes - informs about decisions from paragraphs `11)` and `12)` [point 4. of this chapter](#point-1-4),\
    `10)` Interpretation - informs about decisions from paragraph `6)` [point 4. of this chapter](#point-1-4),\
    `11)` Permits - informs about decisions from paragraph `10)` [point 4. of this chapter](#point-1-4),\
    `12)` Provisions - informs about decisions from paragraph `8)` [point 4. of this chapter](#point-1-4).

6. Special Ruling content can also include the following points (not all points listed must be included in each Special Ruling):

    `1)` Tournament Rules - decisions from paragraphs `1)` and `5)` [point 5. of chapter 1.](#point-1-5),\
    `2)` Referee Committee - decisions from paragraphs `2)` and `7)`-`8)` [point 5. of chapter 1.](#point-1-5),\
    `3)` Thanks and congratulations - decisions from paragraph `3)` [point 5. of chapter 1.](#point-1-5),\
    `4)` Winners - decisions from paragraph `4)` [point 5. of chapter 1.](#point-1-5),\
    `5)` Provisions - informs about decisions from paragraph `8)` [point 5. of chapter 1.](#point-1-5).
