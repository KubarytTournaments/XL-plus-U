<h1 align="center">Referee Committee Regulations</h1>

## 1. Composition of the Referee Committee

1. <a id="point-1-1"></a>The Referee Committee consists of the following functions:

   `1)` Committee Chairman - establishes the composition of the Committee, approves the Tournament Rules, issues Special Rulings and Rulings, assigns tasks to other Committee members, acts as the Organizer, appoints the acting Committee Chairman,\
   `2)` acting Committee Chairman - acts as the Committee Chairman,\
   `3)` Organizer - creates the Tournament Rules, administers the Tournament Page, recruits members of the Referee Committee, issues Rulings, acts as a Referee,\
   `4)` Referee - ensures compliance with the Tournament Rules, helps Players in fulfilling the provisions of the Tournament Rules, issues Rulings,

2. Committee Chairman is Jakub "Kubaryt" Starzyk.

3. Besides the provisions of [point 1. of this chapter](#point-1-1), each member of the Committee:

   `1)` participates in discussions regarding the course of the Tournament, especially if they concern its scope of competence,\
   `2)` performs tasks resulting from their assignment in accordance with the Special Ruling of the Committee Chairman,\
   `3)` performs other tasks assigned by the Committee Chairman,

4. The Committee's composition according to Special Ruling #03-01X:

## 2. Committee's Method of Operation

1. Committee makes decisions in two modes:

   `1)` Through individual decisions - in matters related to the assigned area of the Tournament and matters related to the entire Tournament, which according to the person dealing with it do not require a majority vote,\
   `2)` By majority vote - in matters related to the entire Tournament, appeals against individual decisions and at the request of a Committee member dealing with the area with which the matter is related. Decisions are made by a simple majority.

2. Committee Chairman in the attachment to the Ruling dissolving the Committee includes a report on the work of the Committee, which summarizes its activities during its existence.

## 3. Appeals

1. Each Participant has the right to appeal against any decision of the Committee. The appeal must be made no later than 14 days after the publication of the Ruling in which the decision is included.

2. To appeal against a decision of the Committee, the Participant sends a message on the Discord platform to the Committee Chairman or an email (email address: kubarytcup@gmail.com) with the subject "Appeal against Committee's decision", in which they include information about the decision they are appealing against, justification for the appeal, and any alternative decision that, in their opinion, the Committee should make to replace the decision being appealed against.

3. The Committee, within 7 days of receiving the appeal, reviews the appeal in accordance with point 1 of chapter 2 of these Regulations. In the Ruling informing about the outcome of the appeal, it includes justification for the review of the appeal.
