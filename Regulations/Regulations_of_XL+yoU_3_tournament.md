<h1 align="center">Regulations of XL+yoU 3 tournament</h1>

## 1. Introduction

1. *XL+yoU 3* (hereinafter reffered to as Tournament) is a tournament played in the game Heroes of Might and Magic III with Horn of The Abyss addon (hereinafter reffered to as Heroes III). The purpose of the Tournament is to provide a field for competition for XL+U template players in Heroes III. XL+yoU 3 is a continuation of XL+yoU and XL+yoU 2 tournaments, which took place in 2023 and 2024.

2. Tournament is organized by the Referee Committee (hereinafter reffered to as Committee). The Committee does not make any profit from the organization of the Tournament. All rights to the brand, logo and trademarks of Heroes III are owned by Ubisoft Entertainment SA.

3. Tournament Rules are stated in this document (hereinafter reffered to as Regulations), Documents listed in [point 4. of chapter 1](#point-1-4) and Special Rulings and Rulings of the Referee Committee (Documents, Rulings and Regulations are hereinafter reffered to as Tournament Rules).

4. <a id="point-1-4"></a>Documents that are part of the Tournament Rules:

   - [Privacy Policy](../Documents/XL+yoU_3/Privacy_Policy.md)
   - [Referee Committee Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_3/Referee_Committee_Regulations.md),
   - [Rulings and Special Rulings Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_3/Rulings_and_Special_Rulings_Regulations.md),
   - [Templates Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_3/Templates_Regulations.md)

5. Following hierarchy of the Tournament Rules applies:\
   `1)` Special Rulings,\
   `2)` Regulations,\
   `3)` Documents listed in [point 4. chapter 1.](#point-1-4),\
   `4)` Rulings.

6. Tournament is organized by the Referee Committee (hereinafter reffered to as Committee). The way of functioning of the Committee and its composition is defined in the Referee Committee Regulations.

7. Every time the Tournament Rules mention:

   - **Discord Server**, it should be understood as the H3gg server on the Discord platform [link](https://www.discord.com/invite/WPXRs5aX89),
   - **Participants** or **Participant**, it should be understood, depending on the context: persons participating in any of the phases of the Tournament, persons participating in a given Match or, in the singular: each individual person participating in any of the phases of the Tournament or person participating in a Match,
   - **Match**, it should be understood as all games in the Tournament, between two given Participants, in a given phase of the Tournament,
   - **Game**, it should be understood as a single game in a Match,
   - **Tournament Website**, it should be understood as tournament page on the h3.gg website  [link](htpps://www.h3.gg/),
   - ***Radamantys***, it should be understood as a ranking based on the elo system, generated from the results of tournament matches reported on the h3.gg website and the Liga Heroes website from 10 August 2024 to 10 August 2025. It uses weights for templates, which can be obtained from the Organizers upon request.

8. The Committee reserves the right to change the Tournament Rules in cases where it is necessary for the proper course of the Tournament, by means of a Special Ruling. Changes to the Tournament Rules take effect upon publication, except for changes to the Privacy Policy, which take effect after 14 days from the date of publication of the decision.

9. Polish version of the Tournament Rules is the primary version. In case of a conflict between the English and Polish versions, the Polish version should be followed.

10. The Committee may issue Rulings, which are official decisions in matters of the Tournament, the way of issuing Rulings and their shape is regulated by the Rulings and Special Rulings Regulations.

11. Participants have the right to appeal against any decision of the Committee, no later than 14 days from the publication of the Ruling in which it was included. The way of appeals is defined in the [Referee Committee Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_3/Referee_Committee_Regulations.md).

12. In situations not covered by the Tournament Rules, the Committee's decision is binding. Such decisions will be included in Rulings.

13. Committee reserves the right to refuse a given person to perform the function of commentator or host of the broadcast of the Match played as part of the Tournament. 

## 2. Tournament Format

1. The Tournament will be divided into 2 phases:

   `1)` Registration Phase,

   `2)` Knockout Phase,

2. Registration Phase:

   `1)` The Registration Phase will last from 10 August 2025 to 24 August 2025, during which no Matches will be played,

   `2)` The condition for participation in the Registration Phase is reading and accepting the Tournament Rules, registering on the Tournament Website and joining the Discord Server.

   `2b)` The Committee reserves the right to refuse a given person to participate in the Tournament, in particular if:

   <ul style="list-style: none;">
   <li>a) the person is known for cheating during Matches,</li>
   <li>b) the person is known for high toxicity,</li>
   <li>c) the person is known for lack of respect for fair play rules,</li>
   <li>d) the person has a nickname on the h3.gg website or Discord platform profile that contains:

   - names of publicly known persons (unless it is that person's name),
   - vulgar content, violating good manners, as well as promoting alcohol or other intoxicating substances,
   - misleading names similar to other users' accounts.
   </ul>

   `2c)` The Committee should inform a given person about their exclusion from the Tournament in a private message no later than on the last day of the Registration Phase and include this information in a Ruling,

   `3)` Participation in the Tournament with more than one account is prohibited.

3. Knockout Phase

   `1)` Knockout Phase will start on 24 September 2025 and will last $2 * log_2(n) + 5$ weeks, where `n` is the number of participants rounded up to the nearest power of 2,

   `2)` The condition for joining the Knockout Phase is participation in the Registration Phase, no decision by the Committee excluding from the Tournament and compliance with the Tournament Rules,

   `3)` The Knockout Phase will be divided into Upper Bracket and Lower Bracket, both played in a knockout system, and the Final of the Knockout Phase - a match between the winners of both brackets,

   `4)` In the 1st round of the Knockout Phase, Participants will face each other in pairs, which will be determined on a table established based on *Radamantys*, where the upper half of this table will be seeded according to the order in this table, and the lower part will be seeded randomly, so that each pair will have one Participant from the upper part and one Participant from the lower part,

   `5)` In Upper Bracket Matches will be played in a Bo3 system, in Lower Bracket Matches in a Bo1 system,

   `6)` In case of victory in Upper and Lower Brackets, a Participant advances to the next round of that bracket, in case of defeat in Upper Bracket they advance to Lower Bracket, in case of defeat in Lower Bracket they are eliminated from the Tournament,

   `7)` Each round of Lower Bracket, except for the 1st round, will be divided into 2 steps, in step 1 winners of step 2 from previous round will face losers from previous round of Upper Bracket, in step 2 winners of step 1 will face each other,

   `8)` The deadline for playing a Match in Upper Bracket will be 2 weeks, in Lower Bracket 1 week,

   `9)` The Final of Knockout Phase will be played in a Bo5 system, where winner of Upper Bracket starts with a 1 point advantage, time to play Final of Knockout Phase will be 3 weeks.


4. In case of issues with playing a Match on time:

   `1)` at the request of a Participant of the Match, the Committee may extend the deadline for playing the Match by one day,

   `2)` at the request of both Participants of the Match and with the consent of the Participant with whom the winner of the Match would face, in which there is an issue with playing on time, the Committee may extend the deadline for playing the Match by more than one day, provided that Participants should previously agree on the date of the next round Match with their potential opponent in it, and then provide it to the Committee,

   `3)` at the request of both Participants of the Match, or by decision of the Committee, the tournament system may be changed in a given Match to Bo1, this does not apply to Final of Knockout Phase.

5. In case of issues with agreeing on a date of a Match, the Committee, at the request of one of the Participants of the Match or on its own decision, may set a referee date for a given Match.

6. In case of many issues with playing a Match on time or other circumstances that, in the Committee's opinion, require such a decision, the deadline for each phase may be extended.

3. While loading a Game, the additional time (10 minutes) offered by the Heroes III is ONLY for planning moves. It is prohibited to move heroes, cast spells, buy armies, buildings, etc. during this time.

4. It is prohibited for Participants to excessively prolong the Game and abuse the clock during battles.

5. Conversations between Participants during the Game should be limited to matters necessary to fulfill the provisions of the Tournament Rules, unless both Participants agree to conduct a different conversation.

6. Participants are required to behave politely towards each other during the Game, before and after it on the Heroes III chat, on broadcasts of Games and their chats, in private messages regarding the Tournament and on the Discord Server.

7. It is prohibited for Participants to express discriminatory views, such as queerphobia, racism, sexism, and xenophobia on the Heroes III chat, on Game broadcasts and their chats, in private messages regarding the Tournament and on the Discord Server.

8. It is prohibited for Participants to act to the detriment of the Tournament and the Committee's activities.

9. Participants should refrain from public accusations of violations of the Tournament Rules against others. Any such issue should be reported to the Committee for confirmation.

10. In the Tournament, standard HotA Lobby online rules apply. A link to them can be found [here](https://h3hota.com/en/rules). If a HotA rule contradicts the Tournament Rules, the Tournament Rules take precedence.

11. It is prohibited for more than one person to use a given account in the Tournament.

12. Participants are required to keep Game saves for the duration of the Tournament. Participants are required to provide Game saves and passwords to them at the Committee's request.

13. By registering for the Tournament, the Participant:

   `1)` Accepts the Tournament Privacy Policy,

   `2)` Grants the Committee an inalienable and non-exclusive right to retransmit, record and share selected Games for the purpose of providing commentary and archiving. The Committee may share this right with third parties.

14. Starting from the 1/8 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket, Participants are required to post information about the planned date of the Game on Discord in the `#schedule` channel:

   `1)` In the 1/8 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket, 24 hours before the start of the Game,

   `2)` In the 1/4 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket, 48 hours before the start of the Game,

   `3)` In the finals of the Upper and Lower Bracket and in the Final of the Knockout Phase, 72 hours before the start of the Game.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Committee encourages Participants to post information about the planned date of the Game on Discord in the `#schedule` channel also in earlier phases.

15. Starting from the 1/8 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket, Participants are required to conduct a broadcast of all their Games. The broadcast should be conducted on Twitch, YouTube or on a voice channel on the H3gg Discord Server. The Participant is required to post a link to the broadcast in the `#streams` channel on the Discord Server.

16. If Game is broadcasted by the Committee, the Participant is additionally required to:

   `1)` Set Heroes III resolution in the range from 1180x664 to 1365x768.

   `2)` In the final phases of the Upper and Lower Bracket and in the Final of the Knockout Phase, hide all external overlays, e.g. advertisements, banners, overlays informing about the Game status (so-called *overlay*), visible on the Participant's broadcast,

## 4. Gameplay

1. Games should be played through HotA Lobby available in Heroes III. If HotA Lobby is unavailable, the Game can be played through Hamachi or Game Ranger.

2. Preparation of the Game:

   `1)` One of the Participants creates a room in HotA Lobby (they will be the host), the other Participant joins the room created by the host in the Lobby (they will be the guest).

   `2)` The guest chooses digit 1 or 0, the host uses the `Coin Flip` option to randomly select digit 1 or 0.

3. Template selection: 

   `1)` Templates valid in the Tournament can be found in the [Templates Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_3/Templates_Regulations.md),

   `2)` In case of Bo1 Game, Participants eliminate templates in ABBA system, where A is the winner of the coin flip, until one template remains,

   `3)` In case of Bo3 Game:
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a) the winner of the coin flip chooses the template on which the first Game will be played,

   b) the loser of the coin flip chooses the template on which the second Game will be played,

   c) Participants eliminate templates in BA system, where A is the winner of the coin flip, until one template remains, on which the third Game will be played.
   </ul>

   `4)` Until 1/8 phase of Upper Bracket, in case of Bo3 Game, with both Participants' consent, the Match can be played on one template.

4. Color and town selection:

   `1)` Towns are randomly selected using the `Random Town vs Random Town` option, if two identical towns are selected, the selection should be repeated,

   `2)` Participants can request a reroll of towns and ban 0-2 selected towns for a fee of 500 gold. The first Participant to request a reroll is the winner of the coin flip. Each Participant is entitled to one such ban per Game. If the same pair of towns is selected as before the reroll, the selection should be repeated,

   `3)` The bidding for town and color starts with the Participant who won the coin flip. The first auction concerns towns, the second concerns colors.

   `4)` The winner of the town bidding chooses one of the randomly selected towns,

   `5)` The winner of the color bidding chooses one of the colors: red or blue,

   `6)` The starting hero is chosen first by the red player.

## 5. Restarts

1. Each Participant has two subjective restarts available in the first day of the Game (2 x 111) or one restart in the second day (1 x 112). To use a subjective restart, a Participant should write `111` or `112` on the Game chat, depending on the day they want to use it.

2. First to use a subjective restart is the red player, then the blue player. The blue player has the right to use a subjective restart in the second and third day, counted as a subjective restart from the previous day, up to 5 seconds after accepting the prompt to start their turn.

3. After subjective restarts are used, with the consent of both Participants, or in a situation where they cannot reach an agreement, with the Committee's consent, they can also use technical restarts. Technical restarts can be used if:

   `1)` Some towns has not been generated,

   `2)` The starting hero is different from the one selected in the lobby,

   `3)` Some roads have not been generated,

   `4)` Template settings are different from those listed in point 2. of chapter 2 of the Templates Regulations, except for the settings listed in point 1. of chapter 2 of the Templates Regulations, in which case these settings should be changed before restarting the map,

   `5)` All available expansion roads through road connections are blocked by guards that cannot be bypassed, or bypassing them requires using at least 2000 movement points on each guard, and defeating them causes losses of at least 70% of the starting army (defined as the total HP of the army available at the start and possible to purchase in the first turn of the game) with optimal battle play and assuming neutral morale, for each guard. This does not apply to zone guards,

   `6)` All available expansion roads through road connections are blocked by zone guards with a value of at least 8000, which cannot be bypassed, or bypassing them requires using at least 2000 movement points on each guard, and defeating them causes losses of at least 70% of the army (defined as the total HP of the army available at the start and possible to purchase from dwellings located within a maximum distance of 2000 movement points from the road and possible to occupy by the day of reporting the restart, as well as cities including armies available from dwellings in cities that can be purchased using resources located within a maximum distance of 1500 movement points from the road and possible to collect by the day of reporting the restart) with optimal battle play and assuming neutral morale, for each guard,

   `7)` All available expansion roads through road connections are blocked, some by guards from point `5)` and some by guards from point `6)`.

   - technical restarts can be used up to fifth day of the Game (115), 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Committee recommends that after using subjective restarts, one of the judges should be sent a Game save and passwords to it from both Participants, in order to check the availability of technical restarts on a given generation.

4. After subjective restarts are used, Participants can use early meeting restarts. They are available if there is an interaction between Participants in turn 115 for fast and 116 for medium roads, each Participant has the right to request an early meeting restart, but it is not mandatory. Interaction between Participants is defined as any fight between Participants or taking over an object belonging to the opponent. Early meeting restarts can only be used at the first interaction between Participants in a given generation.

5. None of the restarts can be used to change the selected starting town, starting hero or starting bonus.

## 6. Ending the Match and the Game

1. After the Game is completed, the losing Participant must write "gg" in the Game chat (and in case of playing in a ranked mode, confirm the admission of defeat). This is equivalent to admitting defeat and serves as proof confirming the Game report. The decision to surrender the Game cannot be reverted.

2. Committe at the request of both Participants, or on its own decision, may end the Game by arbitrarily indicating the winner based on the assessment of the situation in the Game.

3. After the Game is completed, Participants are required to give passwords that protect the Game saves.

4. The winner submits a Match report on the Tournament Website, if the losing Participant expresses such a wish, they can submit a report on behalf of the winner.

5. If report was not confirmed within the deadline of the Match and no fraud was reported in the report, it is considered confirmed.

6. Match ends when all reports from its Games are confirmed.

## 7. Punishments

1. The Committee has several options for punishing Participants for violating the Tournament Rules and preparing to violate them. The levels of punishment are defined as follows:

   `1)` Warning - received for minor offenses. The first warning does not carry additional consequences, but each subsequent one does:
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a). Second warning - game forfeit (Match for *Bo1*),

   b). Third warning - disqualification,
   </ul>

   `2)` Game forfeit (Match for *Bo1*) - received for medium offenses, including not adhering to deadlines, may be combined with a warning,

   `3)` Disqualification - received for major offenses,

   `4)` Ban - received for the most serious offenses, equivalent to disqualification and a ban on participating in future editions of the Tournament for a specified or unspecified period.

2. The Committee reserves the right to disqualify a Participant due to a serious offense committed in another Heroes III tournament.

3. Committee in cases where irregularities related to playing a Game occurred, can order repeating it.

4. In case where one of the Participants does not show up for the Game, it is required to follow this procedure (whenever the procedure mentions "you", the Committee means the Participant playing in a given Match who is waiting for their opponent):

   `1)` After 5 minutes from the scheduled time of the Game, inform the Committee about the start of this procedure, and then send a message to your opponent with EXACTLY the following content, omitting quotation marks: “I have attempted to play a match with you, and now I am waiting for you to show up. According to the rules, you have 30 minutes from now to show up and start playing the game, or otherwise you will be penalized with a game forfeit. I have informed the Committee that this procedure has begun.”

   `2)` <a id="point-7-3-2"></a>After 15 minutes from the scheduled time, if your opponent still has not shown up, you must inform the Committee about this situation, and then send a message to your opponent with EXACTLY the following content, omitting quotation marks: “You have not shown up as requested. I have informed the Committee of this situation. I am waiting another 15 minutes for your response, but according to regulations you will be devoid of subjective restarts in this Game.”

   `3)` If your opponent shows up after sending the message from [point 2)](#point-7-3-2) and before 30 minutes have passed since the scheduled time, they will not be able to use subjective restarts in this Game.

   `4)` After 30 minutes from the scheduled time, if your opponent still has not shown up, you must inform the Committee about this situation, and then send a message to your opponent with EXACTLY the following content, omitting quotation marks: “You have not shown up as requested. I have informed the Committee of this situation. I am not waiting for your response any longer.”

   `5)` If your opponent shows up and is willing to start the Game, you should allow them to prepare calmly for the Game. It should be assumed that your opponent should have at least 5 additional minutes to connect to HotA Lobby servers and join the Game room. If your opponent clearly abuses this privilege, you should inform the Committee about it.

   `6)` In case of Matches for which a referee date was set, failure to inform the Committee about a missing Participant may result in a double forfeit.

   `7)` If you want, you can delay the execution of this procedure for up to one hour - but you are not obliged to,

   `8)` While adhering to the above procedure, you are required to prepare evidence in the form of a screenshot showing that your opponent did not show up on time. Additionally, you must prepare evidence in the same form showing the agreed date and time of the Game start. If the deadline was set ambiguously, the Committee may decide not to impose consequences on the missing Participant, depending on the full context. Organizers may also decide to reduce the penalty in case of force majeure.
