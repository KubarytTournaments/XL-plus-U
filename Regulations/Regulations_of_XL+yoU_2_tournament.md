<h1 align="center">Regulations of XL+yoU 2 Tournament</h1>

## 1. Introduction

1. *XL+yoU 2* (hereinafter reffered to as the Tournament) is a tournament played in the game Heroes of Might and Magic III with the Horn of the Abyss addon (hereinafter referred to as Heroes III). The aim of the Tournament is to provide a field for competition for players of XL+U templates in Heroes III. XL+yoU 2 is a continuation of the XL+yoU Tournament, which took place in 2023.

2. Tournament is organized without any profit for the Organizers. All rights to the Heroes III brand, logo and trademarks are owned by Ubisoft Entertainment SA.

3. The basis for the Tournament are the following documents: this document (hereinafter referred to as the Regulations), the documents listed in [point 4. chapter 1.](#point-1-4) and the Rulings and Special Rulings of the Referee Committee (Documents, Rulings and Regulations hereinafter referred to as the Tournament Rules).

4. <a id="point-1-4"></a>Documents that are part of the Tournament Rules:

   - [Privacy Policy](../Documents/XL+yoU_2/Privacy_Policy.md)
   - [Referee Committee Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_2/Referee_Committee_Regulations.md),
   - [Rulings and Special Rulings Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_2/Rulings_and_Special_Rulings_Regulations.md),
   - [Templates Regulations](../Documents/XL+yoU_2/Templates_Regulations.md)

5. Following hierarchy of the Tournament Rules applies:\
   `1)` Special Rulings,\
   `2)` Regulations,\
   `3)` Documents listed in [point 4. chapter 1.](#point-1-4),\
   `4)` Rulings.

6. Tournament is organized by the Referee Committee (hereinafter referred to as the Committee). The way the Committee operates and its composition is regulated by the Referee Committee Regulations.

7. Every time the Tournament Rules mention:

   - **Discord Server**, it should be understood as the H3gg server on the Discord platform [link](https://www.discord.com/invite/WPXRs5aX89),
   - **Participants** or **Participant**, it should be understood, depending on the context: persons participating in any of the phases of the Tournament, persons participating in a given Match or, in the singular: each individual person participating in any of the phases of the Tournament or person participating in a Match,
   - **Match**, it should be understood as all games in the Tournament, between two given Participants, in a given phase of the Tournament,
   - **Game**, it should be understood as a single game in a Match,
   - **Tournament Website**, it should be understood as tournament page on the h3.gg website  [link](htpps://www.h3.gg/),
   - ***Radamantys***, it should be understood as a ranking based on the elo system, generated from the results of tournament matches reported on the h3.gg website and the Liga Heroes website from 24 June 2023 to 24 June 2024. It uses weights for templates, which can be obtained from the Organizers upon request.

8. Committee reserves the right to change the Tournament Rules at any time, by Special Ruling.

9. Polish version of the Tournament Rules is the basic version. In case of discrepancies between the English version and the Polish version, the Polish version should be followed.

10. Committee may issue Rulings, the way of issuing Rulings and their form is regulated by the Rulings and Special Rulings Regulations.

11. In situations not covered by the Tournament Rules, the decision of the Committee is binding. Such decisions will be included in the Rulings.

12. Committee reserves the right to refuse a given person to perform the function of commentator or host of the broadcast of the Match played as part of the Tournament.

## 2. Tournament Format

1. Tournament will be divided into 3 phases:

   `1)` Registration Phase,

   `2)` Group Phase,

   `3)` Knockout Phase,

2. Registration Phase:

   `1)` Registration Phase will last from 14 June 2024 to 28 June 2024, no games will be played in this phase,

   `2)` the condition for participation in the Registration Phase is to read and accept the Tournament Rules, sign up for the Tournament and join the Discord Server,

   `2b)` Committee reserves the right to exclude a person from participating in the Tournament, in particular if:

   <ul style="list-style:none">
   <li>a) the person is known for cheating during games,</li>
   <li>b) the person is known for high toxicity,</li>
   <li>c) the person is known for lack of respect for fair play rules,</li>
   <li>d) the person has a nickname in the profile on the website <a href="https://www.h3.gg">h3.gg</a> or the Discord platform:

   - containing the names of publicly known people (unless it is the name of that person),
   - containing vulgar content, violating good manners, as well as promoting alcohol or other intoxicating substances,
   - misleading fe. names similar to the accounts of other users.</li>
   </ul>

   `2c)` about the exclusion from the Tournament, the Committee should inform the person in a private message, no later than on the last day of the Registration Phase, and include information about it in the Ruling,

   `3)` it is forbidden to participate in the Tournament with more than one account.

3. Group Phase:

   `0)` In the case of less than 128 Participants signing up for the Tournament, point 3 of this chapter does not apply, and the Tournament will start from the Knockout Phase,

   `1)` Group Phase will start on 28 June 2024 and will last until 19 July 2024,

   `1b)` in case of the need to play overtimes, the duration of the phase may be extended by a week,

   `2)` the condition for participation in the Group Phase is participation in the Registration Phase, no decision by the Committee to exclude from the Tournament and compliance with the provisions of the Regulations,

   `2b)`<a id="point-2-3-2b"></a>16 Participants will be exempted from participation in this phase, selected based on *Radamantys*,

   `3)` in the Group Phase, Participants will be randomly divided into groups, with groups of equal size as much as possible,

   `4)` the average number of Participants assigned to a group will be determined after the end of the registration phase based on the number of registered Participants, but will not exceed 6,

   `5)` the games will be played in a round-robin system within groups,

   `6)` Matches will be played in a Bo1 system,

   `7)` for a win, the Participant receives 1 point, in case of a loss it is 0 points, in case of a draw the Match should be repeated,

   `8)` two Participants with the highest number of points in each group become the winners of the phase,

   `9)` in case of two or more Participants in one group having the same number of points, the result of the direct Matches between these Participants decides,

   `10)` <a id="point-2-3-10"></a>in case the result of the direct Matches does not allow to unambiguously indicate the winners (e.g. let three Participants A, B and C be in one group and have the same number of points, Participant A won the direct Match with Participant B, B with C, and C with A) tiebreakers should be played,

      `11)` in case of a repeat of the situation from [paragraph 10).](#point-2-3-10), the Committee will decide on the order in the group by tossing a coin in accordance with [paragraph `12)`](#point-2-3-12),

   `12)` <a id="point-2-3-12"></a>the coin toss is carried out by a member of the Committee in the presence of all Participants concerned. The entire procedure should be recorded by a member of the Committee.

4. Knockout Phase:

   `1)` in case of more than 128 Participants signing up for the Tournament, the Knockout Phase will start on the day after the end of the Group Phase and will last, depending on the total number of Participants registered for the Tournament, from 19 to 23 weeks,

   `1b)` in case of less than 128 Participants signing up for the Tournament, the Knockout Phase will start on 24 June 2024 and will last, depending on the total number of Participants registered for the Tournament, from 19 to 23 weeks,

   `2)` the condition for joining the Knockout Phase is winning the Group Phase or exclusion from the Tournament in the Group Phase in accordance with [paragraph `2b)` of point 3 of this chapter](#point-2-3-2b),

   `2b)` in case of less than 128 Participants signing up for the Tournament, the condition for participation in the Knockout Phase is participation in the Registration Phase, no decision by the Committee to exclude from the Tournament and compliance with the provisions of the Regulations,

   `3)` the Knockout Phase will be divided into the Upper Bracket and Lower Bracket, both will be played in a knockout system, and the Final of the Knockout Phase, between the winners of both brackets,

   `4)` in the 1st round of the Knockout Phase, Participants will face each other in pairs, which will be determined based on the places taken in the previous phase, in the second place the number of points scored in the previous phase, and in the third place based on *Radamantys*, covering the period from 24 June 2023 to the end of the group phase,

   `4b)` two Participants who played in the same group in the Group Phase cannot play against each other in the first round of the Knockout Phase,

   `4c)` Participants mentioned in [paragraph `2b)` of point 3 of this chapter](#point-2-3-2b), will be treated as Participants who took 1st place in the group and scored 1 more point than the maximum number of points in the largest group,

   `4d)` in case of less than 128 Participants signing up for the Tournament, in the 1st round of the Knockout Phase, Participants will face each other in pairs, which will be determined based on *Radamantys*,

   `5)` in the Upper Bracket, Matches will be played in a Bo3 system, in the Lower Bracket in a Bo1 system,

   `6)` in case of victory in the Upper and Lower Bracket, the Participant advances to the next round of the given bracket, in case of defeat in the Upper Bracket, the Participant drops to the Lower Bracket, in case of defeat in the Lower Bracket, the Participant is eliminated from the Tournament,

   `7)` each round of the Lower Bracket, except for the 1st round, will be divided into 2 steps, in the 1st step the winners of the 2nd step of the previous round will face the losers of the previous round of the Upper Bracket, in the 2nd step the winners of the 1st step will face each other,

   `8)` the deadline for playing the Match in the Upper Bracket will be 2 weeks, in the Lower Bracket 1 week,

   `9)` the Final of the Knockout Phase will be played in a Bo5 system, where the winner of the Upper Bracket starts with a 1 point advantage, the deadline for playing the Final of the Knockout Phase will be 3 weeks.

5. By mutual agreement of both Participants, a Bo1 Match can be played in a Bo3 system, and a Bo3 Match in a Bo5 system.

6. About the deadlines depending on the number of Participants, the Committee will inform in the Ruling opening the Group Phase.

7. In case of problems with playing the Match on time:

   `1)` at the request of the Match Participant, the Committee may extend the deadline for playing the Match by one day,

   `2)` at the request of both Match Participants and with the consent of the Participant who would face the winner of the Match in which there is a problem with playing on time, the Committee may extend the deadline for playing the Match by more than one day, provided that the Participants should agree on the date of the Match in the next round with their potential opponent in it, and then provide it to the Committee,

   `3)` at the request of both Match Participants, or by the decision of the Committee, the system of the Match may be changed to Bo1, this does not apply to the Final of the Knockout Phase.

8. In case of problems with finding a common date for the Match, the Committee, at the request of one of the Match Participants or on its own decision, may set a referee date for the given Match.

## 3. Fair Play, Required Consents and Requirements for Conducting the Game

1. Using crashes to gain an advantage is prohibited. After loading the map due to a Heroes III error, the Participant must repeat their moves on the adventure map and in battles in the turn in which the error occurred.

2. External help. There are many ways to get additional information useful in the ongoing Game. Below is a list along with whether a given method is allowed:

   | Method of obtaining information | Its legality in the Tournament |
   | :---                          | :----                         |
   | Tips (a third party provides the Participant with information that can be deduced by watching only his gameplay) by text | Allowed, if the hint does not have a significant impact on the Game and does not cause the Participant to change his game plan. Participants using this type of advice, if they do not meet the requirements mentioned in the 1st sentence, may be punished, each such case should be reported to the Committee; Participants broadcasting the Game should remove violating Tournament Rules hints, preferably through moderation, |
   | Tips by phone, teamspeak, skype, etc. talking live | Prohibited |
   | Spoilers (a third party provides the Participant with information that cannot be deduced by watching only his gameplay) | Prohibited - if the Participant reads the spoiler, he should play as if he had not seen it, and the person providing the spoiler should be muted by the player or chat moderator, for the duration of the Game |
   | Ghosting (presence on the opponent's stream or shoutcast of this Game) | Prohibited |
   | Programs reading the memory of Heroes III - for example, to see the undiscovered part of the map or predict the development of a hero | Prohibited |
   | Value or damage calculators | Allowed |
   | Video tutorials | Allowed |
   | Watching your own stream or recording of a given Game during its duration | Prohibited |
   | Screenshots from the ongoing Game | Prohibited |
   | Wiki pages | Allowed |
   | Own notes about Heroes III or the Game | Allowed |
   | Macros and binds | Allowed are macros and binds limited to simulating a combination of 2 keys, without additional delays and without cursor movements. |
   
3. When loading the Game, the additional time (10 minutes) offered by the Game is used EXCLUSIVELY for planning moves. It is forbidden to move heroes, cast spells, buy armies, buildings, etc.

4. Participants are required to behave politely towards each other during the Game, before and after the Game on the Heroes III chat, on the broadcasts of the Games and on their chats, in private messages regarding the Tournament and on the Discord Server.

5. Participants are prohibited from expressing discriminatory views, such as queerphobia, racism, sexism and xenophobia on the Heroes III chat, on the broadcasts of the Games and on their chats, in private messages regarding the Tournament and on the Discord Server.

6. Participants should refrain from publicly accusing others of violating the Tournament Rules. Any such problem should be reported to the Committee for confirmation.

7. In Tournament, standard HotA Lobby online rules apply. The link to them can be found <a href="https://h3hota.com/en/rules" target="__blank">tutaj</a>. If a rule of the HotA regulations contradicts the Tournament Rules, the Tournament Rules take precedence.

8. Signing up for the Tournament, the Participant:

   `1)` Accepts the Privacy Policy of the Tournament,

   `2)` Grants the Committee an irrevocable and non-exclusive right to retransmit, record and share selected Games for the purpose of providing commentary and archiving. The Committee may share this right with third parties.

9. Starting from the 1/8 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket, the Participant is obliged to provide information about the planned date of the Game on Discord on the `#schedule` channel:

   `1)` In the 1/8 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket 24 hours before the start of the Game,

   `2)` In the 1/4 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket 48 hours before the start of the Game,

   `4)` In the finals of the Upper and Lower Bracket Phases and in the Final of the Knockout Phase 72 hours before the start of the Game.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Committee encourages to provide information about the planned date of the Game on Discord on the `#schedule` channel also in earlier phases.

10. Starting from the 1/8 phase of the Upper and Lower Bracket, the Participant is obliged to broadcast all of their Games. The broadcast should be conducted on the Twitch, YouTube platform or on the voice channel on the H3gg Discord Server. The Participant is obliged to post a link to the broadcast on the `#streams` channel on the Discord Server.

11. If the Game is retransmitted by the Participant, the Participant is additionally obliged for the duration of the Game:

   `1)` Set the resolution of Heroes III in the range from 1180x664 to 1365x768,

   `2)` in the finals of the Upper and Lower Bracket Phase and the Final of the Knockout Phase, hide all external overlays, e.g. advertisements, banners, overlays informing about the state of the Game (so-called *overlay*) visible on the Participant's broadcast.

12. Using one account by more than one person in the Tournament is prohibited.
  
## 4. Gameplay

1. Games should be played through the HotA Lobby available in Heroes III. In case the HotA Lobby is not available, the Game can be played through Hamachi or Game Ranger.

2. Game preparation:

   `1)` one of the Participants creates a room in the HotA Lobby (he will be the host), the second Participant joins the room created by the host in the Lobby (he will be the guest),

   `2)` the guest chooses the number 1 or 0, the host flips a coin through the `Coin Flip` option, drawing the number 1 or 0.

3. Template selection:

   `1)` the templates applicable in the Tournament can be found in the [Templates Regulations](../Dokumenty/XL+yoU_2/Regulamin_Szablonów.md),

   `2)` in the case of a Bo1 Match, Participants eliminate templates in the ABBA system, where A is the winner of the coin flip, until one template remains,

   `3)` in the case of a Bo3 Match:
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a) the winner of the coin flip chooses the template on which the first Game of the Match will be played,

   b) the loser of the coin flip chooses the template on which the second Game of the Match will be played,

   c) Participants of the Match eliminate templates in the BA system, where A is the winner of the coin flip, until one template remains, on which the third Game will be played.
   </ul>
4. Color and town selection:

   `1)` Drawing towns is done by the Random Town vs Random Town option, in case of drawing two identical towns, the drawing must be repeated,

   `2)` Participants can demand a reroll of towns and ban 0-2 drawn towns for a fee of 500 gold coins. The winner of the coin flip declares the first *reroll*. Each Participant is entitled to a maximum of one such ban during the Game. In case of drawing the same pair of towns as before the *reroll*, the drawing must be repeated,

   `3)` The bid for the town and color starts with the Participant who won the coin flip. The first bid refers to the towns, the second to the color,

   `4)` The winner of the town bid chooses one of the drawn towns,

   `5)` The winner of the color bid chooses one of the colors: red or blue,

   `6)` The starting hero is chosen by the red player first.

## 5. Restarts

1. Each Participant has two subjective restarts in the first day of the Game (2 x 111) or one restart in the second day (1 x 112). To use a subjective restart, the Participant must write `111` or `112` on the Heroes III chat, depending on the day, in which the Participant wants to use it.

2. First decision on the subjective restart is made by the red player, then the blue player. The blue player has the right to make a decision on the subjective restart in 112 and 113, counted as a subjective restart from the previous one, up to 5 seconds from accepting the prompt to start the turn.

3. After using subjective restarts, with the consent of both Participants, or in a situation where they cannot come to an agreement, with the consent of the Committee, they can also use technical restarts. Technical restarts can be used if:

   `1)` a town is not generated,

   `2)` the starting hero is different from the one chosen in the lobby,

   `3)` roads are not generated,

   `4)` the template settings are different from those listed in point 2. chapter 2 of the Regulations of Templates, with the exception of the settings listed in point 1. chapter 2 of this, in this case, before restarting the map, change these settings,

   `5)` all expansion roads through road connections are blocked by guards that cannot be bypassed, or their bypass requires the use of at least 2000 movement points per guard, and their defeat results in losses of at least 70% of the starting army (defined as the total HP of the army available at the start and possible to buy in the first turn of the game) with the most optimal battle and assuming neutral morale, per guard. This does not apply to zone guards,

   `6)` all expansion roads through road connections are blocked by zone guards of at least 8000 value, which guards cannot be bypassed, or their bypass requires the use of at least 2000 movement points per guard, and their defeat results in losses of 70% of the starting army (defined as the total HP of the army available at the start and possible to buy in the first turn of the game) with the most optimal battle and assuming neutral morale, per guard,

   `7)` all expansion roads through road connections are blocked, some by guards from paragraph `5)` and some by guards from paragraph `6)`.

   - technical restarts can be used at any time during the Game, but no later than the 5th day (115),

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Committee recommends to, after using subjective restarts, send a Game save to the Committee, in order to check the availability of technical restarts on a given generation.

4. After using subjective restarts, Participants can use early encounter restarts. They are available if there is an interaction between Participants in turn 115 for fast roads and 116 for medium roads, each Participant has the right to demand an early encounter restart, but it is not mandatory. Interaction between Participants is defined as any fight between Participants or taking over an object belonging to the opponent. Early Encounter Restarts can be used only after first interaction between Participants on given generation.

5. None of the restarts can be used to change the starting hero or starting town or starting bonus.

## 6. Ending the Match and the Game

1. After playing the Game, the loser writes `gg` on the Heroes III chat (and in the case of playing in the ranking mode confirms the loss), this is tantamount to admitting defeat and evidence confirming the report from the Game. The decision to surrender the Game cannot be reverted.

2. After the end of the Game, Participants are obliged to provide the passwords protecting the Game saves.

3. Winner submits a report from the Game on the Tournament Website, a the request of intereset party, loser can submit a report for the winner.

4. If report is not confirmed in Match deadline or fraud is not reported in the report, it is considered confirmed.

5. Match ends when all reports from the Game are confirmed.

## 7. Punishments

1. Committee has at its disposal several options in which it can punish Participants for violating the Tournament Rules and making preparations to violate them. Levels of punishment are defined as follows:

   `1)` Warning - received for minor offenses. The first warnings do not carry additional consequences, but each subsequent one does:
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a) Second warning - walkover in the Game (Match in the case of Bo1),

   b) Third warning - disqualification,
   </ul>

   `2)` Walkover in the Game (Match in the case of Bo1) - received for medium offenses, including not keeping deadlines, can be combined with a warning,

   `3)` Disqualification - received for major offenses,

   `4)` Ban - received for the biggest offenses, is equivalent to disqualification and a ban on participating in the next editions of the Tournament for a specified period of time, or not.

2. Committee in cases where irregularities related to playing a Game occurred, can order repeating it.

3. If one of the Participants fails to show up on the scheduled date of the Game, the following procedure should be used (whenever the procedure includes phrases in the second person, the Committee mean the Participant playing in a given Game who is waiting for his late opponent):

   `1)` After 5 minutes from the scheduled time, inform the Committee that you are starting this procedure, then message your opponent on private message with EXACTLY the following text, without quotes: “I have attempted to play a Game with you, and now I am waiting for you to show up. According to the rules, you have 15 minutes from now to show up and start playing the game, or otherwise you will be penalized with a game forfeit. I have informed the Committee that this procedure has begun."

   `2)` <a id="point-7-3-2"></a>After 15 minutes from the scheduled time, if the opponent has not shown up yet, you have to inform the Committee of this situation, then message your opponent with EXACTLY the following text, without quotes: “You have not shown up as requested. I have informed the Committee of this situation. I am waiting another 15 minutes for your response, but according to regulations you will be devoid of subjective restarts in this Game.”

   `3)` If the opponent does show up after sending the message in [paragraph `2)`](#point-7-3-2) and before the 30 minutes after scheduled time, they won't be able to use subjective restarts in this Game.

   `4)` After 30 minutes from the scheduled time, if the opponent has not shown up yet, you have to inform the Committee of this situation, then message your opponent with EXACTLY the following text, without quotes: “You have not shown up as requested. I have informed the Committee of this situation. I am not waiting for your respone any longer.”

   `5)` If the opponent does show up and is willing to start the Game immediately, you should allow for the necessary setup time. If the opponent is visibly stalling for time, you should report this to the Committee. As a rule of thumb, you should allow for at least 5 extra minutes to let the opponent connect to the online lobby and join the room.

   `6)` If this was a Game scheduled by the Committee, and you neglect to inform the Committee about the fact that your opponent is missing, a double game forfeit may be given at Committee discretion.

   `7)` You may delay this procedure up to an hour if you wish to give more time to your opponent - but you are not required to do so.

   `8)` For the procedure listed above, you have to provide proof in the form of a screenshot that you have messaged your opponent and they have not responded on time. Additionally, you should provide the proof of the scheduled date and time for the Game. If this procedure was used for an ambiguously scheduled Game, the Committee may decide to not penalize the missing player depending on the full context. The Organizers may also decide to reduce the severity of the penalty in case of force majeure.

4. Participtant can appeal against the Committee decision up to the day of the end of the phase following the phase in which they received the penalty.
